# rupuchiru_sousaku

## About This Repository
This repository contains source documents for the fictional worldbuilding project.

The archive is intended for creative writing, collaborative worldbuilding, documentation, and AI-assisted research.

Documents are primarily provided in plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) formats.

## Fictional Content Notice
All civilizations, species, states, religions, ideologies, historical events, and conflicts described in this archive are part of a fictional setting unless explicitly identified otherwise.

Some documents may contain descriptions of:

Warfare, war crimes, mass violence, and human rights abuses.
Authoritarian governments, discrimination, persecution, and extremist ideologies.
Religious conflicts, political propaganda, and oppressive social systems.
Biological reproduction, reproductive anatomy, mating, fertilization, pregnancy, and childbirth.
These subjects are presented for fictional, historical, biological, or encyclopedic documentation.

The inclusion of such material does not constitute endorsement of violence, discrimination, persecution, or extremist ideologies.

Descriptions of reproductive biology and anatomy are intended for scientific and worldbuilding purposes, not sexual arousal.

## AI Access and Document Updates
This repository is also used to evaluate access to fictional source documents through AI assistants and external integrations.

Documents may be updated as the fictional setting develops. AI-generated summaries and interpretations should distinguish documented facts from speculation.

## 創作内容に関する注意事項
本リポジトリに収録される文明、種族、国家、宗教、思想、歴史的事件、紛争などは、特に明記されない限り架空世界の設定です。

資料には、以下の内容が含まれる場合があります。

戦争、戦争犯罪、大規模な暴力、人権侵害
独裁体制、差別、迫害、過激な思想
宗教的対立、政治的宣伝、抑圧的な社会制度
生殖、生殖器の構造、交接、受精、妊娠、出産などの生物学的説明
これらは架空世界の歴史、社会制度、生物学などを記録・解説するためのものです。
